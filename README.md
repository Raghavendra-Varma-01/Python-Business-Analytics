# Python Data Analytics Projects

A collection of Python-based data analytics projects focused on data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, statistical analysis, and data visualization.

## Projects

### 1. AAL Sales Analysis

**Folder:** `Project-1-AAL-Sales-Analysis`

Analyze three months of US clothing sales data to identify daily and monthly trends, state-wise performance, customer group behaviour, and time-based sales patterns.

**Practical Use:** Supports sales monitoring, inventory planning, marketing decisions, and store performance analysis.

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Scikit-learn.

### 2. Marketing Campaign Analysis

**Folder:** `Project-2-Marketing-Campaign-Analysis`

Analyze customer demographics, product spending, purchasing channels, and marketing campaign responses to understand customer behaviour.

**Practical Use:** Supports customer segmentation, campaign evaluation, and data-driven marketing decisions.

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn.
